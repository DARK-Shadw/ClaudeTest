    How to play

    Goal: be the first to empty your hand — or be the last player standing. Hit 25 cards and you're out (Mercy Rule)!

    Matching: play a card with the same colour, number or symbol as the top card. Wild cards go on anything.

    No playable card? Keep drawing until you get one, then play it (or keep it).

    Stacking: when someone hits you with a draw card, answer with another draw card of equal or higher value (+2, +4, +6, +10, Wild Reverse +4) to pass the pain on. Otherwise you eat the whole stack and lose your turn.

    • Skip – next player loses a turn. Skip Everyone – you play again.
    • Reverse – flips direction (acts like a skip with 2 players).
    • Draw 2 / Draw 4 – coloured penalty cards.
    • Discard All – dump every card of that colour from your hand.
    • Wild Reverse +4 – pick a colour, reverse and +4. Wild +6 / +10 – pick a colour, next player draws.
    • Wild Color Roulette – pick a colour; next player draws until they hit it, keeps them all and loses their turn.
    • 7 – swap hands with anyone. 0 – everybody passes their hand in the direction of play.
